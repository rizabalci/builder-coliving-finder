# Builder Coliving Finder — Deep Research Version

A tighter, more directive version optimized for autonomous deep-research agents
that browse the web on their own. Use this when the tool runs long multi-step
research rather than a single answer.

---

```
Research live, currently operating "builder coliving" programs worldwide and
return a ranked, sourced report.

DEFINITION
Residential communities where founders, builders, engineers, and remote workers
live together and work on their own projects, with shared living and working
space. Two categories, label each:
  (A) Structured residencies / cohorts (fixed dates, application, sometimes
      demo days or investment)
  (B) Open-enrollment coliving you can join anytime while keeping outside work

INCLUDE founder houses, hacker houses, AI builder villas, startup residencies
with housing, and coliving + coworking communities for entrepreneurs and remote
tech workers.

EXCLUDE generic hotels, hostels, plain Airbnbs, pure coworking with no housing,
and anything defunct or not accepting people in 2026.

PRIORITIZE these regions, but go worldwide: Southeast Asia, Latin America,
Southern Europe, Georgia, Turkey. Flag pricier hubs (SF, NYC, Bangalore, London)
separately.

KEY FILTERS (mark every result against these)
- Allows outside work / remote consulting income: yes / no / unclear
- Monthly all-in cost, ideally $1,500 to $3,000 per person
- Visa access for a Turkish passport holder, plus the local digital-nomad visa
- Internet quality and timezone vs Central European Time

METHOD
- Search broadly, then verify each candidate is still operating in 2026 by
  checking its own site or recent posts. Drop or flag stale entries.
- Prefer primary sources (the program's own site, application page) over listicles.
- Do not invent pricing, dates, or links. If unverified, say so.

DELIVERABLE
1. Comparison table of 15 to 20 verified matches: name, type, location, monthly
   cost, allows outside work, visa ease, apply link.
2. Ranked top 5 with a short rationale each.
3. A "could not verify" list of promising names you found but couldn't confirm.
4. Full source citations.
```
