# Builder Coliving Finder — Generic Version

Personal details stripped out so anyone can use it. Fill in the bracketed
placeholders with your own situation.

```
ROLE & GOAL
Act as a relocation and community researcher. Find currently operating programs
worldwide that match a specific concept: residential communities where builders,
founders, engineers, and remote workers live together and work on their own
projects, sharing both living space and a workspace.

WHAT COUNTS (the concept)
- Founder / builder / hacker houses (live and build together)
- AI builder villas and startup residencies with a residential component
- Coliving + coworking communities aimed at entrepreneurs, founders, indie
  hackers, and remote tech workers
- Curated villa shares or "build houses" with a community of people shipping
  products or startups

Include BOTH types and clearly label each:
(A) Structured residencies / cohorts: fixed dates, application, sometimes demo
    days or investment
(B) Open-enrollment commercial coliving: join anytime, keep your own work

EXCLUDE
- Generic hotels, hostels, or Airbnbs with no community or work focus
- Pure coworking with no living component (unless paired with on-site or partner
  coliving, in which case note it)
- Anything defunct or not accepting people in 2026

MY PROFILE (edit these to match yourself)
- Work: [your work, e.g. remote software engineer]. I need to KEEP working during
  the stay, so flag whether each program ALLOWS outside work and side income.
- Budget: roughly [$X to $Y] per month all-in per person. [Willing / not willing]
  to share to lower the per-head cost.
- Passport / residence: [your citizenship and any residence permit]. Factor in
  visa access for my passport and flag places that are hard to stay in long term.
- Lifestyle I value: [e.g. warm climate, surfing, nightlife, quiet, nature].
- Language: [languages you speak or want to use].

WHERE TO LOOK
Worldwide, but prioritize: [list your preferred regions or write "no preference"].
Also surface notable builder-house hubs in pricier places and label them clearly.

FOR EACH PROGRAM, RETURN
1.  Name and one-line description
2.  Type: (A) structured residency or (B) open coliving
3.  Location: city, country, and specific area
4.  Community focus
5.  Pricing and what's included
6.  Duration, cohort dates, or minimum stay
7.  Application or booking process, selectivity, and a direct link
8.  Does it allow outside work / income? (yes / no / unclear)
9.  Visa reality for my passport, plus any digital-nomad visa
10. Internet quality and timezone
11. Community vibe and any events or demo day component
12. Source link(s)

OUTPUT FORMAT
- A comparison table of the top 12 to 20 matches with key columns.
- A ranked shortlist of the 5 best fits, with 2 to 3 sentences each on why.
- Be current as of 2026 and cite sources. If you can't verify something is still
  operating, say so rather than guessing.
```
